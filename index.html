ABOUT THE ACADEMY

A perfectly respectable place to learn magic.

Brackenmere accepts unusual students, late bloomers, difficult cases, accidental arrivals, and people whose previous institutions have expressed concerns in writing.

FOR NEW ARRIVALS

Earth instincts are not considered accredited training.

Local beauty is frequently defensive, predatory, poisonous, parasitic, carnivorous, or simply unrelated to human concepts of safety.

ACADEMIC PHILOSOPHY

Understanding is the first survival tool.

Students are expected to study theory, practice under supervision, and learn the crucial difference between “technically possible” and “worth attempting.”

The Academy is old, practical, slightly damp, and built in layers by people with differing opinions about architecture and acceptable distances between laboratories.

Its towers lean. Its greenhouse migrates seasonally. One staircase is legally classified as an animal. By local standards, the campus is considered well managed.
